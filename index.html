Weather Effects

Seven zero-dependency canvas weather animations in pure JavaScript. No libraries, no assets, works offline — even from file://.

🌧️Rain

Slanted rain streaks with splashes, lightning bolts and thunder flashes. Adjustable angle and intensity.

❄️Snow

Four parallax layers with depth-of-field blur, wind gusts and snow settling on the ground.

🌤️Sky

Day sky with volumetric clouds, dawn-to-night phases, a sun that dims behind cover and passing bird flocks.

🌫️Fog

Layered strata fog drifting with the wind, with a hazy sun breaking through.

🧊Hail

Ice stones that bounce and scatter, shed chips on impact and melt on the ground under a green storm sky.

💨Wind

Gust lines that sweep through in bursts, curl mid-air and dissolve, with tumbling autumn leaves.

🌨️Sleet

Mixed rain, half-melted flakes and ticking ice pellets that build a slush layer on the ground.
