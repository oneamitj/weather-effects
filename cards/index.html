Weather Cards

The same canvas effects, used the way an app would actually use them: a clock card with the local sky living behind it. Every card shows the real current time for its city, and the sky, clouds and precipitation are drawn live — no images, no video, no libraries.

Time of day Weather intensity App theme

Switch the time of day and every card crossfades to the new light — sun position, sky colour, haze and cloud shading all move together.

Clear, partly cloudy, cloudy

The three base skies. Cloud cover thickens from left to right, and the sun dims whenever a cloud drifts in front of it.

Rain and thunderstorms

Rain falls over the cloudy sky. The storm card adds lightning that lights up the whole card for a moment when it strikes.

Snow

Flakes drift down in parallax layers and settle into a drift along the bottom edge.

Hail

A thunderstorm with hail: stones bounce off the bottom of the card, shed ice chips on impact and melt where they land.

Sleet

Rain, half-melted flakes and ice pellets falling together, slowly building a slush line across the bottom.

Fog

Fog banks drift across the sky and pool near the ground, thin enough to still let the light through.

Wind

Gusts arrive in bursts, curl through the card and fade, carrying leaves with them. Wind layers on top of any other condition instead of replacing it.

Compact rows

The same cards at list height, for a saved-locations screen. The effects scale down with the card so the detail still reads at 58 pixels tall.
